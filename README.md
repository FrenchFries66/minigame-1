# Minigame 1
## Devlog
Xiangyi Li, she/her

I think Unity's structure works like putting on a stage play. A Scene is the stage itself; GameObjects are the actors and props on it, and Components are the scripts and costumes handed to each actor, giving them behavior and appearance. The play only comes to life when every actor knows their role, just as a game world only works when each GameObject has the right Components attached.
